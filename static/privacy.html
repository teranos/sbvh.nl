Last updated: 17 October 2026

SBVH operates several services. This policy states how SBVH handles personal data in all of them. What differs per service — which providers process the data, and for how long it is kept — is in the annex at the bottom, one section per service.

Scope

This policy applies to every service SBVH operates, including the ones named in the annex. Where a service publishes its own statement — because it addresses consumers in their own language, or because national law requires it — that statement governs that service and this one covers the rest. Clean Amsterdam is such a service; see the annex.

What we process

By category, rather than by field, so this stays true as services change:

  • Identity and contact — name, email address, phone number, and whatever you choose to tell us when you get in touch.
  • Content you send us — documents, messages, and files you upload or send, and text extracted from them.
  • Service records — appointments, orders, invoices, and the correspondence around them.
  • Technical and usage data — device and browser details, timestamps, and which features were used. On websites this is collected only with consent, except for first-party measurement that keeps no identifier past the visit. Where a service does that, its annex entry says so.
  • Advertising identifiers — only where a service runs advertising, and only with consent.

Why, and on what legal basis

  • Performance of a contract (GDPR art. 6(1)(b)) — to do the work you asked for, schedule it, and invoice it.
  • Legal obligation (art. 6(1)(c)) — tax and administrative retention.
  • Consent (art. 6(1)(a)) — analytics, marketing, advertising measurement, and any processing a service asks you to agree to. You can withdraw it at any time, and withdrawing it does not affect what was lawful before.
  • Legitimate interest (art. 6(1)(f)) — keeping services running and secure.

Who processes it besides us

SBVH does not sell personal data and does not share it beyond what is needed to run a service. Processors act on our instruction and under a data processing agreement. They fall into these roles, and the annex names the current ones per service:

  • Hosting and storage
  • Identity and sign-in
  • Analytics and advertising measurement
  • Communication — email, messaging, push notifications
  • Automated analysis of content you send

Beyond processors, data goes to authorities only where the law requires it.

Retention

  • Invoices and bookkeeping: seven years, the Dutch fiscal retention period.
  • Contract and service records: for the duration of the relationship, then at most two years.
  • Everything else: as long as the service needs it, and no longer. Per-service periods are in the annex.

On deletion of an account, or withdrawal of the consent a processing rests on, the data is deleted within 30 days except where a retention period above requires otherwise.

Transfers outside the EU

Some providers process data in the United States. Where they do, they are certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or covered by Standard Contractual Clauses.

Security

Data is held on systems with disk encryption, access control and current software. It is not sent over unsecured channels.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you
  • Have inaccurate data corrected
  • Have your data deleted
  • Restrict or object to processing
  • Receive a copy of your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent

Write to privacy@sbvh.nl. We respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you can lodge a complaint with the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Changes

This policy is updated when a service changes what it does. The version carrying the date at the top is the one in force.

Annex — services

sbvh.nl

This website.

  • Measurement: the page visited, the site that sent you there, campaign (UTM) parameters, which link was clicked, and a random identifier that lasts for one browser tab and is gone when the tab closes. No identifier is kept across visits, no IP address or browser details are stored with it, and nothing is shared. It runs on SBVH's own QNTX server at api.q.sbvh.nl, hosted by Amazon Web Services in Frankfurt; nobody else receives it.
  • Legal basis for measurement: legitimate interest (art. 6(1)(f)) in knowing which pages and links are used. Because nothing outlives the visit, no consent is asked for.
  • Retention: kept as the record of visits to the site. Once the tab is closed, nothing in it leads back to a person.
  • Account: booking a call asks you to sign in first. Signing in with Google gives SBVH your name, email address and profile picture, and creates an account for you in the SBVH namespace on the same QNTX server. Your browser keeps a key of its own and a session, so the next visit does not ask again.
  • Bookings: the time you chose, what you wrote about the call, your name and email address, and when a call was moved or cancelled. A confirmation is mailed to you and to SBVH through Amazon Simple Email Service in Frankfurt.
  • Processors for the account and bookings: Google (sign-in) and Amazon Web Services (hosting and mail).
  • Legal basis for the account and bookings: taking steps at your request before entering into a contract (art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Retention for the account and bookings: for as long as the account exists. Bookings are kept as the record of the calls arranged.

Werf

A job matching service.

  • Processes: name and email from LinkedIn sign-in via AWS Cognito; CV documents and text extracted from them; push notification tokens; usage timestamps.
  • Processors: AWS (hosting, Cognito sign-in), LinkedIn (sign-in), and an AI provider for CV parsing.
  • Legal basis: consent.
  • Retention: for as long as the account exists; deleted within 30 days of account deletion or withdrawal of consent.
  • Recruiters cannot access your information without your explicit permission.

Clean Amsterdam

A cleaning company trading under SBVH, addressing consumers in the Netherlands. It publishes its own statement in Dutch, English, French and German, covering cookies, the Meta and Google tags, and the retention periods that apply to cleaning work: cleanamsterdam.nl/privacy.html. That statement governs Clean Amsterdam.

Advertising

SBVH runs paid campaigns for its own services on Meta platforms, through Meta's Marketing API.

  • Processes: campaign, budget and delivery data belonging to SBVH's own advertising account. No personal data of visitors or applicants is sent to Meta through this route.
  • Audience measurement on a service's own website is covered by that service's statement and happens only with consent.
  • Processor: Meta Platforms Ireland Limited, under its own privacy policy.
